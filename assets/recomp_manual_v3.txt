## Training plan — researched & corrected

  
What changed from v2 and why: The previous plan had each muscle hit only once per week (Push Mon, Pull Tue, Legs Thu). Research consensus is that hitting each muscle group 2× per week produces significantly more hypertrophy than 1× per week at the same total volume (Schoenfeld et al. 2016, replicated multiple times). With the available equipment (max 26 kg barbell, 9.5 kg per-hand dumbbells), an Upper/Lower 4-day split hits this frequency, keeps individual sessions manageable, and leaves Mon/Wed/Fri for conditioning — matching the original schedule intent.

  

  
    
 | Day | Focus | Muscle groups hit 2× this week | Duration

    
 | Mon | Upper A (push emphasis) | Chest, shoulders, triceps, back, biceps | 50–60 min

    
 | Tue | Lower A + core | Quads, hamstrings, calves, glutes, abs | 50–60 min

    
 | Wed | Zone 2 conditioning | Heart, lungs, active recovery | 30–35 min

    
 | Thu | Upper B (pull emphasis) | Back, biceps, chest, shoulders, triceps | 50–60 min

    
 | Fri | Lower B + HIIT + core | Quads, hamstrings, glutes, abs | 50 min

    
 | Sat | Active recovery walk | — | 20–30 min

    
 | Sun | Full rest + meal prep | — | —

## Progression rules

  

    
 | Condition | Action

    
 | Form is rough on any lift | Stay at current load — form first, always

    
 | Hit top of rep range on all sets, 2 sessions in a row | Add smallest plate available (2 kg) next session

    
 | Bar maxed (26 kg) and reps still easy | Add 3-sec lowering → then 2-sec pause → then extra set → then single-arm/single-leg variant

    
 | Pull-ups: 4 × 10 strict reps clean | Add +2 kg via loaded backpack (books/water bottles)

    
 | Push-ups: 3 × 25 easy | Feet on bench (decline) → one-arm negative → archer push-up

    
 | 3+ weeks without strength progress | Check sleep and calorie intake first before adding volume

  

  
Is the workout enough? Yes — for someone with 3 months of prior gym experience, now back after a gap: 4 resistance sessions/week hitting each muscle 2×, plus 2 conditioning sessions, is well above the minimum effective dose for hypertrophy. Volume per muscle group is 10–14 sets/week, which sits squarely in the optimal range (10–20 sets/week per Schoenfeld 2017). The limiting factor now is execution quality and progressive overload, not adding more sessions.
