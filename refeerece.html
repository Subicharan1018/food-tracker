Working load capacity — this is what the numbers in the training plan below assume: dumbbells split evenly across two hands ≈ 9.5 kg/hand for two-handed lifts (curls, presses), or up to 19 kg loaded on one handle for single-arm work and goblet holds. Lunch has to survive ~6 hours unrefrigerated in the bag before it's eaten, so every lunch recipe below is a dry preparation — no dal, no curd, no watery gravy. Turmeric and chili also act as natural preservatives, which is why classic Tamil Nadu travel foods (puliyodharai, sundal) lean dry and well-spiced. Always let food cool fully before packing it — sealing it hot is what actually causes spoilage, not the heat outside.

Recipes

Breakfast — rotate through the week (4 eggs + 4 chapatis, ~700 kcal / 37g protein / 21g fat / 74g carb each)

Vengaya Muttai Poriyal — onion egg scramble

Ingredients

4 eggs, 1 small onion (finely chopped), 1 green chili (slit), ¼ tsp turmeric, salt, a few curry leaves, 1 tsp oil.

Method

Heat oil, splutter curry leaves, sauté onion and chili 2–3 min till soft. Add turmeric and salt, crack in the eggs, scramble on medium heat 2–3 min until just set. Serve with hot chapatis.

Milagu Omelette — cracked pepper omelette

Ingredients

4 eggs, ½ tsp crushed black pepper, 2 tbsp chopped coriander, 1 small onion (finely chopped), salt, 1 tsp oil.

Method

Whisk everything together. Pour into a hot pan with the oil, cook flat-omelette style, about 2 min per side.

Boiled eggs + tomato-onion kachumber

Ingredients

4 eggs, 1 tomato + 1 onion (finely chopped), a squeeze of lemon, pinch of salt and pepper.

Method

Boil eggs 8–9 min, peel and halve. Mix the tomato, onion, lemon, and salt as a quick side. Fastest option for rushed mornings.

Kalakki Muttai Curry — thick scrambled egg curry

Ingredients

4 eggs, 1 onion, 1 tomato, ¼ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp chili powder, ½ tsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp oil, coriander leaves.

Method

Sauté onion till golden, add ginger-garlic, tomato, and spices, cook to a thick masala (3–4 min). Pour in beaten eggs and stir-scramble into the masala until set.

Lunch protein — rotate Mon to Fri. All dry preparations, safe unrefrigerated for ~6 hours

Naatu Kozhi Milagu Varuval — pepper chicken dry roast (Mon)

~180g raw chicken · ~230 kcal / 38g protein / 7g fat / 2g carb
Ingredients

180g boneless chicken (cubed), 1 tsp crushed pepper, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, curry leaves, 1 tsp oil, salt.

Method

Marinate chicken with turmeric, salt, ginger-garlic for 15 min (can batch-marinate on Sunday). Air-fry at 200°C for 12–15 min, shaking halfway, or pan-roast in the oil on medium-high 8–10 min until browned and dry. Toss through crushed pepper and curry leaves at the end.

Cool completely before packing — never seal hot food into the lunch box.

Soya Chunks Milagu Varuval — pepper soya dry fry (Tue)

50g dry soya chunks · ~180 kcal / 26g protein / 5g fat / 12g carb
Ingredients

50g dry soya chunks, 1 onion, ½ tsp pepper, ½ tsp turmeric, curry leaves, 1 tsp oil, salt.

Method

Rehydrate soya chunks in hot water 10 min, then squeeze out excess water firmly (important for shelf life). Sauté onion in oil, add soya chunks and spices, dry-roast 6–8 min until the edges crisp slightly.

Cheapest protein of the set — good default for tight weeks.

Vella Kadalai Sundal — white chickpea sundal (Wed)

60g dry chickpeas · ~210 kcal / 12g protein / 6g fat / 30g carb
Ingredients

60g dry white chickpeas (soaked overnight, pressure-cooked 3–4 whistles till soft), mustard seeds, 1 dry red chili, curry leaves, 2 tbsp grated coconut, 1 tsp oil, salt.

Method

Temper mustard seeds, red chili, and curry leaves in the oil. Add the cooked chickpeas, toss with coconut and salt, and let any excess moisture cook off for 2–3 min.

The classic temple-prasadam dish — proven to sit unrefrigerated for hours. Lower protein than the chicken days, so pair it with a spare boiled egg if you want the protein target closer.

Paneer Chettinad Varuval — dry paneer fry (Thu)

100g paneer · ~280 kcal / 18g protein / 20g fat / 10g carb
Ingredients

100g paneer (cubed), 1 onion, 1 tomato, ½ tsp chettinad masala (or fresh-ground fennel, pepper, cinnamon), 1 tsp oil, curry leaves, salt.

Method

Lightly sear the paneer cubes in a dry pan or air-fry 5 min till the edges firm up. Separately cook onion, tomato, and spices into a thick dry masala, then toss the paneer through to coat.

Keep it dry-fried, not in gravy — gravy paneer doesn't hold well in a bag.

Kozhi Chukka — chicken chukka with coconut bits (Fri)

~180g raw chicken · ~250 kcal / 38g protein / 9g fat / 5g carb
Ingredients

180g chicken, 1 onion, curry leaves, 2 tbsp fresh grated coconut, ½ tsp chili powder, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp oil, salt.

Method

Marinate and cook the chicken as in the Monday recipe. In the last 2 minutes, toss through sautéed onion, coconut bits, and curry leaves for a drier, coconutty finish.

Carrot-beans poriyal, cabbage poriyal, or beetroot poriyal — same method for all: chop the vegetable fine, temper mustard seeds, a pinch of urad dal (for tempering only, not a dal dish), and curry leaves in ½ tsp oil, add the vegetable with a splash of water, cover and cook 5–6 min until just tender and dry, finish with grated coconut. Cool before packing.

Dinner — chicken curry rotation (220g chicken + 3 chapatis, ~820 kcal / 55g protein). Eaten fresh at home, so gravy is fine.

Naatu Kozhi Kuzhambu — country chicken curry (Mon / Thu)

Ingredients

220g country chicken, 1 onion, 2 tomatoes, 3 tbsp grated coconut (ground to a paste), 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, curry leaves, 1 tsp oil.

Method

Sauté onion till golden, add ginger-garlic and tomatoes, cook down. Add spice powders, then the chicken, and sear 3–4 min. Add the coconut paste and enough water for a medium gravy, simmer covered 15–20 min till the chicken is tender.

Milagu Kozhi Varuval — pepper chicken roast (Tue / Fri)

Ingredients

220g chicken, 1½ tsp crushed pepper, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, curry leaves, 1 tsp oil, salt.

Method

Marinate 15 min, then air-fry at 200°C for 15–18 min, shaking halfway, or pan-roast in the oil until dry and browned.

Chettinad Kozhi Curry (Wed)

Ingredients

220g chicken, 1 onion, 1 tomato, 1 tsp chettinad masala (fennel, pepper, star anise, cinnamon, dry red chili — ground fresh), 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, curry leaves, 1 tsp oil.

Method

Sauté onion and ginger-garlic, add tomato and the ground masala, cook till fragrant. Add chicken, sear, then add water for a thick gravy and simmer 15–20 min.

Kozhi Chukka — dry chicken chukka (Sat)

Ingredients

220g chicken, 1 onion, 3 tbsp grated coconut, curry leaves, ½ tsp chili powder, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp oil.

Method

Cook chicken with spices in minimal water till tender, then let the liquid cook off completely, tossing through the coconut and curry leaves for a dry finish. Lighter, quicker cook for an active-recovery day.
